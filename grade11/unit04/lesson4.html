Tips · page 47 · to give your opinion in a discussion

Opinion → question → polite response

  • 🗣️

    State it

    I think … · I believe … · In my opinion, …

  • ❓

    Ask

    What about you? · Why do you think so?

  • 🤝

    Respond

    I agree with you. · (I’m afraid) I have to disagree.

Task 3 · Pairs · the most important skill or experience 9 min

Defend your choice — then switch roles

Student A

Choose one skill. Give your opinion and a reason. Ask for your partner’s view.

Student B

Ask: Why do you think so? Agree or disagree politely. Give one reason.

In my opinion, teamwork skills are the most important because participants must solve problems together. What about you?
