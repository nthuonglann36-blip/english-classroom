Practice

Ms Hoa, Mark and Mai · Tasks 1–4

20 minutes
Task 1 · Listen and read 5 min 🎧 Track 10 · 2:12

What is a generation gap? · 1–2

Ms HoaGood morning, class. Today, we’ll talk about the generation gap. So, what is the generation gap?
MarkWell, I think it’s the difference in beliefs and behaviours between young and older people.
Task 1 · Listen and read

What is a generation gap? · 3–4

Ms HoaYou’re right, Mark. So, is there a generation gap in your family?
MarkNot really, Ms Hoa. I live in a nuclear family with my parents and brother. We understand each other quite well though we still argue over small things.
Task 1 · Listen and read

What is a generation gap? · 5–6

Ms HoaRight. We can’t avoid daily arguments. What about you, Mai?
MaiWell, I live with my extended family, and I have to learn to accept the differences between the generations.
Task 1 · Listen and read

What is a generation gap? · 7–8

Ms HoaThat’s a good point. Can you give us an example?
MaiWell, my grandparents hold traditional views about male jobs and gender roles. For example, my grandfather wants my brother to be an engineer, but my brother hopes to become a musician. …
Task 1 · Listen and read

What is a generation gap? · 8–9

Mai… And my grandmother thinks women have to do all the housework.
Ms HoaHow about your parents? Do they share your grandparents’ views?
Task 1 · Listen and read

What is a generation gap? · 10

MaiNo, they don’t. They think that we should follow our dreams. They give us advice, but never force us to follow in their footsteps.
Task 1 · Listen and read

What is a generation gap? · 11

Ms HoaThank you, Mai, for sharing your experiences. You must respect your parents and grandparents, but you should also express your opinion. Now let’s continue our discussion with …

Task 3 · Find words or phrases in Task 1 5 min

First team with the correct spelling wins a point

1. all people who were born at about the same time generation

2. a family that consists of a father, a mother, and children nuclear family

3. a family with parents, children, uncles, aunts, grandparents, etc. extended family

4. disagreements about things arguments

Task 4 · Choose the modal verbs used in Task 1 5 min

must · have to · should

1. Mai lives with her extended family, and she has to / should learn to accept the differences. has to

2. Mai’s grandmother thinks women have to / should do all the housework. have to

3. Mai’s parents believe that children must / should follow their dreams. should

4. Ms Hoa thinks that children have to / must respect their parents and grandparents. must
