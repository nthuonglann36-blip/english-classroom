Warm-up

Can you make it shorter?

5 minutes
Pair work · what is a contracted form?

we will → we’ll

The apostrophe ( ’ ) shows that letters are missing.

we will → we’ll

you are → you’re

cannot → can’t

that is → that’s

they do not → they don’t

let us → let’s

I am → I’m

who will → who’ll

Grammar

must · have to · should

13 minutes
Remember! · page 20

must or have to?

must

necessary now — the speaker decides

I must clean my room before my friend comes.

mustn’t = not allowed

You mustn’t behave rudely.

have to

necessary — someone else decides

I have to clean my room every day. (My mother forces me.)

don’t have to = not necessary

They don’t have to wear uniforms at the weekend.

Remember! · page 20

Past, future — and advice

  • Past · had to

    My grandmother had to do all household chores.

  • Future · will have to

    We will have to accept these generational differences.

  • Advice · should

    I think parents should limit their children’s screen time.

Why had to, not “musted”? must has no past form — we use had to.

Task 1 · Circle the correct answers 6 min

Choose the modal verb

1. You must / mustn’t respect older people. You can learn valuable lessons from them. must

2. We don’t have to / have to wear uniforms on weekdays. It’s the rule at our school. have to

3. You should / mustn’t ask your parents for permission if you want to colour your hair. should

4. In the past, women had to / must do all housework. It was one of their duties. had to
