2

Before listening

New words + Task 1

10 minutes
Vocabulary · 1–2

Words you will hear

  • disagreement (n) /ˌdɪs.əˈɡriː.mənt/ sự bất đồng
  • upset (v) /ʌpˈset/ làm buồn, làm khó chịu
Vocabulary · 3–4

Words you will hear

  • complain (about) (v) /kəmˈpleɪn/ phàn nàn (về)
  • allow sb to do sth /əˈlaʊ/ cho phép ai làm gì
Vocabulary · 5

Words you will hear

  • appearance (n) /əˈpɪə.rəns/ ngoại hình
Task 1 · About you · always, sometimes or never? 5 min

Compare with your partner

1. My parents complain about my clothes and hairstyle.

2. My parents let me colour my hair.

3. My parents limit the time I spend on electronic devices.

4. My parents take away my electronic devices after 9 p.m.

5. My parents control everything I do.

“My parents sometimes … What about yours?”

3

While listening

Kevin and Mai

20 minutes
Task 2 · Number the things in the order they are mentioned 8 min 🎧 Track 13 · 2:15

Listen for the order of ideas

a. Kevin’s parents limit his screen time. 4

b. Mai asks Kevin if he has any disagreements with his parents. 3

c. Mai is not allowed to colour her hair. 2

d. Mai’s choice in clothes and hairstyle upsets her mother. 1

Task 3 · NO MORE THAN TWO words 12 min 🎧 Track 14 · 2:14

Listen again and answer

1. What does Mai’s mother keep complaining about? Mai’s appearance

2. Where doesn’t Mai’s mother allow her to wear tight jeans? at school

3. What does Kevin mainly use his smartphone and laptop for? (his) homework

Task 3 · NO MORE THAN TWO words

Listen again and answer

4. According to Mai, what might Kevin’s parents worry about? his eyesight

5. What time do Kevin’s parents take away his smartphone and laptop? 10 p.m.

Tip

Underline the key words · predict the type of answer · check: 1–2 words only.

4

After listening

Do you agree?

8 minutes
Task 4 · Groups · give a reason and an example

Do you agree with Mai’s mother and Kevin’s parents? Why / Why not?

I agree / disagree because … · I think parents should / shouldn’t … · In my opinion, teenagers need …

Group challenge

Agree on ONE piece of advice for parents and ONE for teenagers.
