Warm-up · brainstorm together

Benefits of each path

🔧 Vocational training

  • may cost less
  • shorter study duration
  • practical, job-ready skills
  • can earn while learning (apprenticeship)
  • high demand for skilled trades

🎓 Academic study

  • wider career choices
  • higher earning potential long-term
  • develops critical thinking
  • research skills and academic network
  • prestigious degree for certain jobs

Vocabulary · SAY IT · SEE IT · USE IT

Six key terms

  • benefit /ˈbenɪfɪt/ (n) an advantage or helpful result — lợi ích
  • practical skills /ˈpræktɪkl skɪlz/ (n) abilities that help you do real-world tasks — kỹ năng thực hành
  • critical thinking /ˌkrɪtɪkl ˈθɪŋkɪŋ/ (n) the ability to analyse and evaluate ideas carefully — tư duy phản biện
  • research skills /rɪˈsɜːtʃ skɪlz/ (n) abilities to find, analyse and use information — kỹ năng nghiên cứu
  • flexible programme /ˈfleksəbl ˈprəʊɡræm/ (n) a course that can be adjusted to suit your needs — chương trình linh hoạt
  • admission requirements /ədˈmɪʃn rɪˈkwaɪəmənts/ (n) the qualifications needed to enter a school or university — điều kiện tuyển sinh

Task 2 · Choose A–D for the four missing turns

Complete the conversation about vocational training

A It's the cost. Vocational training is much cheaper.

B I couldn't agree more. Practical skills are so useful.

C OK. Let's discuss how long each path takes.

D Now, let's think about one more benefit.

  1. Linh: "I think the first benefit is practical skills. Nam: ________" D — Now, let's think about one more benefit.
  2. Nam: "What do you think is the second benefit?" Linh: "________" A — It's the cost. Vocational training is much cheaper.
  3. Nam: "Good point! The third benefit?" Linh: "________" C — OK. Let's discuss how long each path takes.
  4. Nam: "Right. Vocational courses are shorter." Linh: "________" B — I couldn't agree more. Practical skills are so useful.

Tasks 3–4

Talk about academic study · who is each option for?

15 minutes
Task 3 · work in pairs · pick three benefits and agree on them

Talk about academic study

Idea bank: develop critical thinking · improve research skills · study a wide range of subjects · become more independent · get a prestigious degree · better career opportunities · higher salary in the long run · flexible study programmes · join clubs and societies · network with professors and peers

With your partner, agree on the THREE most important benefits of academic study. Monitor for correct grammar. Encourage use of "I think…", "What about…?", "I agree". 3 minutes pair, then share with class.

Task 4 · discuss learners, not just the courses

Who is each option for?

  • 🔬

    The Researcher

    I love experiments and studying scientific theories.

    Academic study — needs critical thinking, research skills, university degree
  • 🔧

    The Builder

    I enjoy fixing things and working with my hands.

    Vocational training — practical skills, trade, apprenticeship
  • 👩‍⚕️

    The Healer

    I want to become a nurse or a pharmacist.

    Academic study — requires formal degree from a medical institution
  • 🍽️

    The Chef

    I dream of owning a restaurant one day.

    Vocational training — hands-on cooking, apprenticeship with a skilled chef

Production

60-second group pitch

7 minutes
Group discussion · build your recommendation

Use the decision map to report a balanced opinion

01 Interests What do you enjoy doing?

02 Strengths What are you good at?

03 Goals What career do you want?

04 Best fit Our group recommends… because…

One speaker per group. Three reasons. One conclusion. Our group recommends vocational training for learners who prefer hands-on activities. First, it develops practical skills quickly. Second, it costs less than a university degree. Third, students can do an apprenticeship and earn a salary while learning. In conclusion, vocational training is a great fit for people who want to start working sooner.

Exit ticket · two minutes

What can you do now?

  1. Name one practical benefit of vocational education. Develops practical skills / shorter study / cheaper / apprenticeship available.
  2. Name one academic benefit of university study. Develops critical thinking / research skills / wider career options / prestigious degree.
  3. How do you agree in a discussion? I agree with you. / I couldn't agree more. / That's a good point. Also…
