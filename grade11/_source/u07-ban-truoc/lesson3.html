Vocabulary · SAY IT · SEE IT · USE IT

Words 1–5

  • opportunity /ˌɒpəˈtjuːnəti/ (n) a chance to do or achieve something — cơ hội
  • independently /ˌɪndɪˈpendəntli/ (adv) without depending on others — một cách độc lập
  • hands-on /ˌhændzˈɒn/ (adj) gained by doing practical activities, not just reading — thực hành trực tiếp
  • salary /ˈsæləri/ (n) money paid regularly to someone for their work — lương (tháng)
  • formal /ˈfɔːml/ (adj) received through organised classes and lectures — chính quy, học ở trường
Vocabulary · SAY IT · SEE IT · USE IT

Words 6–9

  • manage /ˈmænɪdʒ/ (v) to succeed in doing something difficult — xoay xở, làm được
  • trade /treɪd/ (n) a skilled job requiring practical training, e.g. plumber, electrician — nghề thủ công / kỹ thuật
  • apprenticeship /əˈprentɪʃɪp/ (n) training in which you learn a skill while working alongside an expert — hình thức học nghề có lương
  • institution /ˌɪnstɪˈtjuːʃn/ (n) an established organisation, e.g. a university or college — cơ sở giáo dục, tổ chức

Paragraph A · read and decide who this path might suit

Route A: Going to college or university

Higher education suits learners who enjoy studying academic subjects and want to work in fields that require a university degree. At a university or college, students study a wide range of subjects in their chosen field. They also have opportunities to study and work independently, as well as do research. Most courses last three to five years. After graduation, students get a degree that helps them find good career opportunities.

Paragraph B · read and decide who this path might suit

Route B: Getting vocational training

Vocational education prepares learners who prefer practical learning and want to learn a specific trade. Students can study at vocational schools or training centres. The programmes often include hands-on activities that help students practise job skills. Some students do an apprenticeship, which allows them to earn a salary while learning from experts. Graduates can manage the real tasks that employers require and find jobs more quickly than students from formal educational institutions.

Which learner fits which route?

  • 🔬

    Learner A

    I enjoy maths and want to conduct research.

    University — academic subjects + research opportunities
  • 🍳

    Learner B

    I love cooking and want to start working soon.

    Vocational — hands-on skills + apprenticeship + quicker route to work
  • 💡

    Learner C

    I'm good at fixing electrical systems.

    Vocational — learn a trade (electrician)
  • 📚

    Learner D

    I want to become a doctor or a lawyer.

    University — requires a formal degree and academic study

Which path would YOU choose? Give two reasons using vocabulary from today. Encourage use of: formal / hands-on / degree / trade / apprenticeship / opportunity. Pair → share.
