Now that I'm a (1) ________, I face many choices. Some of my friends have already decided to pursue (2) ________ at university, while others prefer to do an (3) ________ to learn a trade. Review: Pronunciation

Rise ↗ or fall ↘ — question intonation

Pronunciation review · Textbook Looking Back · read aloud then check the arrow

Mark the intonation

  1. Are you interested in studying at university? ↗ (Yes/No question — rising)
  2. How much is the fee for vocational training? ↘ (Wh- question — falling)
  3. Have you made a decision yet? ↗ (Yes/No question — rising)
  4. What kind of qualification will you need? ↘ (Wh- question — falling)
  5. Is vocational training a good option? ↗ (Yes/No question — rising)
  6. Where will you study after graduation? ↘ (Wh- question — falling)

Review: Grammar

Perfect participle vs. perfect gerund

10 minutes
Grammar review · same form, DIFFERENT jobs

Quick reminder

Perfect participle clause

Having + V3, main clause

= an earlier completed action

Having finished school, my brother travelled.

Perfect gerund

Having + V3 as subject or object

= a completed action (noun function)

Having finished school made him proud.

Grammar review · Textbook Looking Back · rewrite the sentences

Rewrite using a perfect participle clause or perfect gerund

  1. After he finished school, my brother took a year off and travelled around the world. Having finished school, my brother took a year off and travelled around the world. participle clause
  2. She gained a qualification in cooking. This helped her find a job at a restaurant. Having gained a qualification in cooking, she found a job at a restaurant. participle clause
  3. I didn't study hard enough, so I failed the entrance exam. Not having studied hard enough, I failed the entrance exam. negative participle clause
  4. He worked as an apprentice for two years. It made him very experienced. Having worked as an apprentice for two years made him very experienced. perfect gerund as subject
