2

Vocabulary

3 words in context

8 minutes
Vocabulary · LOOK → SAY → UNDERSTAND → USE

Three key words

  • professional /prəˈfeʃənəl/ (adj) connected with a job that needs special training or skills — chuyên nghiệp, có chuyên môn
    In context: a professional cooking course trains you to work in a restaurant kitchen.
  • brochure /ˈbrəʊʃə(r)/ (n) a small booklet giving information about a school or service — tờ rơi, sách giới thiệu
    In context: The school sent us a brochure about their cooking courses and fees.
  • hands-on /ˌhændzˈɒn/ (adj) involving real practice, not just reading or theory — thực hành trực tiếp
    In context: The course is hands-on — you cook every day, not just study recipes.
Vocabulary check · say each answer aloud, then explain your clue

Spot the correct word

  • Clue 1 A learner working with a skilled chef apprentice — a person learning a trade by working alongside an expert
  • Clue 2 A booklet explaining course options at a cooking school brochure
  • Clue 3 The type of training that involves cooking real meals hands-on training

Pre-listening

Task 2 — check the meanings

5 minutes
Pre-listening · Task 2 · choose A or B for each highlighted word

Check the meanings in context

  1. Professional cooking course: A. connected with real situations and time. B. connected with a trained occupation. B — professional = connected with a trained occupation
  2. Apprentice: A. a person learning a trade by working alongside an expert. B. a teacher who works in a kitchen. A — apprentice = a person learning a trade by working alongside an expert
  3. Brochure: A. a long textbook about cooking. B. a short booklet giving information about a course. B — brochure = a short booklet giving information

Task 4 · Listening for detail · listen again · fill in each gap (no more than TWO words)

ABC Vocational School — school notes

SHORT COURSE: Duration: 2–3 (1) ________

PROFESSIONAL COURSE: For (2) ________ cooks / trains (3) ________

AFTER THE COURSE: Students receive a (4) ________

CAREER: Can apply for jobs (5) ________

Answers: 1 — months · 2 — professional · 3 — restaurant cooks · 4 — certificate · 5 — immediately

Post-listening

Would you try a cooking course? Role-play: Open Day

10 minutes
Post-listening · choose one course and justify your opinion

Which course would you choose?

🍳 Short Course

Duration: 2–3 months

Explore a new hobby and gain practical skills

👨‍🍳 Professional Course

Trains you to work as a restaurant cook

Earn a certificate upon completion

Apply for jobs immediately after

Which course would you recommend to a friend who wants to become a chef? Why? I would recommend the professional course because it provides specialist training for restaurant cooks and gives a certificate. Having completed the course, my friend could apply for jobs immediately.

Speaking extension · pair role-play · Vocational School Open Day

At the Open Day — role-play

Student A · Visitor

You are a school-leaver interested in cooking.

Ask about: course types · duration · cost · certificate

Use: Could you tell me…? / What about…? / How long…?

Student B · Receptionist

You work at ABC Vocational School.

Explain: short course (2–3 months) · professional course · certificate · job opportunities

Use: We offer… / After completing the course… / I'd recommend…

Switch roles after 3 minutes. Monitor for correct use of indirect questions (Lesson 6 preview). Give feedback on politeness and fluency.
