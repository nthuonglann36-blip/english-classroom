Pre-listening

Are you an independent learner?

4 minutes
Task 1 · Tick (✓) the columns about you · compare your answers in pairs 4 min

3 “yes” answers or more = an independent learner!

YesNo
1. Are you interested in learning new things?
2. Do you set your learning goals and make plans to achieve them?

Post-listening

Which characteristics do you have?

13 minutes
Task 4 · Groups · discuss the following questions 10 min

Which characteristics of independent learners do you have? Which one do you want to develop?

🙋 We are all … (self-motivated · responsible · hard-working · curious)

🎯 … wants to develop … because these skills will help …

🗣️ Report a summary of your group’s discussion to the class.

Task 4 · sample report

Hi, I’m Khoi.

There are three students in our group: Minh, Quang, and me. We are all self-motivated, responsible, hard-working, and curious. However, we think independent learners need more skills.

Minh wants to develop critical thinking skills because these skills will help her make better decisions. Quang needs time-management skills to get more done in less time. I’d like to be more self-confident so as to deal with challenges, overcome difficulties, and better communicate with people.

Wrap-up · exit ticket 3 min

Before you leave…

2. What helps independent learners achieve their goals? (detailed) study plans

3. One characteristic you want to develop, and why? Students’ own answers
