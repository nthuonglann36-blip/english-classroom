Pronunciation

Intonation in invitations, suggestions, and polite requests

12 minutes
Remember! · page 87

The fall-rise ↘↗ sounds friendlier

Direct

Give me your phone.

→ sounds like an order

Polite request ↘↗

Could you show me your phone, please? ↘↗

We usually use fall-rise intonation in invitations, suggestions, and requests. It makes them sound friendlier and more polite.

Grammar

Cleft sentences with It is/was … that/who …

13 minutes
Remember! · page 88

Cleft sentences

🔦 A cleft sentence is used to focus on a particular part of the sentence and to emphasise what we want to say.

➡️ We use preparatory It to introduce the thing that we want to focus on while the rest is put in a relative clause introduced with a relative pronoun, e.g. that or who.

📌 Nam taught Mai how to use the app in the library last weekend. (no particular focus)

Remember! · page 88 · examples

Move the spotlight

It was Nam that/who taught Mai how to use the app in the library last weekend. (focus on Nam)

It was the app that Nam taught Mai how to use in the library last weekend. (focus on the app)

It was in the library that Nam taught Mai how to use the app last weekend. (focus on the library)

It was last weekend that Nam taught Mai how to use the app in the library. (focus on last weekend)

Grammar · Task 1 · Rewrite the sentences using cleft sentences focusing on the underlined parts 6 min

Rewrite the sentences (1–2)

1. John is saving his pocket money to buy a new phone.
→ It ________ to buy a new phone. It is John that/who is saving his pocket money

2. He gets 20 dollars every week by doing chores around the house.
→ It ________ by doing chores around the house. It is 20 dollars that he gets every week
