Pronunciation

Weak forms · contracted forms · linking

12 minutes
Listen and complete the conversation 🎧 Track 24 · 0:42

Living in a skyscraper

A: What’s it like living (1) in a skyscraper?

B: (2) It’s great. I (3) can enjoy the best views (4) of the city from my sofa.

A: (5) Do you have a balcony?

B: No, (6) I don’t. (7) But I have huge windows.

Underline the weak forms · circle the contracted forms · mark the linking (‿)

Then practise in pairs

A: What’s‿it like living‿in‿a skyscraper?

B: It’s great. I can /kən/ enjoy the best views‿of the city from my sofa.

A: Do /də/ you have‿a balcony?

B: No, I don’t. But‿I have huge windows.

blue = weak form · ◯ = contracted form · ‿ = linking

Grammar 2 · Choose the correct words or phrases 5 min

Linking verbs and stative verbs

1. You look so angry / angrily. What’s wrong? angry

2. I think / I’m thinking of taking up yoga. I feel a bit stressed / stress these days. I’m thinking · stressed

3. Do you remember / Are you remembering Ms Wilson? She’s a great yoga instructor. Do you remember

4. The traffic gets / is getting worse. What do you think / are you thinking we should do? is getting · do you think
