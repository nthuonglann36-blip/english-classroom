1. What is the life expectancy in many of the richest countries? A

A. More than 80. · B. Less than 80. · C. More than 85.

2. What was the life expectancy in Japan in 2022? C

A. Over 94. · B. About 84. · C. Over 85.

Listening 2 · Listen again · choose A, B or C

Listen for details

3. According to the speaker, what is the first reason for the increased life expectancy? B

A. People get access to better food. · B. Better medical care is available. · C. People live in a better environment.

4. What do people do when they realise the importance of good health? B

A. They continue unhealthy habits such as smoking and drinking. · B. They stop doing things that are bad for their health. · C. They ignore what they eat or drink every day.

Listening 2 · Listen again · choose A, B or C

Listen for details

5. What is the benefit of understanding age-related diseases better? C

A. People can live as long as they want. · B. People can treat their diseases. · C. People can take better care of themselves.

Tip

Watch out for paraphrases: over = more than · get access to = be available · break bad habits = stop doing things that are bad

I totally agree … · I can’t agree more. · Our group chooses … because …

Speaking 2 · report to the class

In our group, we all agree that …

In our group, we all agree that green space with more pedestrian zones and cycle paths is the most important thing. When there are more pedestrian zones and cycle paths, city dwellers will want to exercise more. This will help them stay healthy and live longer.
