Warm-up

What would you say?

5 minutes
Pair work · match each situation with a message

What would you say?

Why don’t you…? · Don’t forget to… · Can you…? · I’m pleased to…

1. Invite a friend to your birthday party. Why don’t you come to my party?

2. Remind your friend to return a book. Don’t forget to return my book.

3. Ask your friend to bring some fruit. Can you bring some fruit?

4. Accept an invitation. I’m pleased to come.

Task 1 · the message in order

Three parts of a short message

1 · Greeting Hi Linda,

2 · Main body Thanks for lending me your book about healthy cooking. It’s great! How about coming to my house this Sunday? We can try some recipes from the book. Can you bring some fresh mangos from your garden? We’ll need them for one of the recipes.

3 · Closing See you soon, Mai

While writing

Tasks 2–3

16 minutes
Task 2 · Begin with the words given 6 min

Write a sentence for each message

1. invite someone to your birthday party next week → Why don’t you come to my birthday party next week?

2. suggest joining the reading club with you → How about … How about joining the reading club with me?

3. remind someone to return a book to the library → Don’t forget to … Don’t forget to return the book to the library.

Task 2 · Begin with the words given

Write a sentence for each message

4. accept an invitation to your friend’s birthday party → I’m pleased to … I’m pleased to come to your birthday party.

5. ask if you have to dress formally for the party → Do … Do I have to dress formally for the party?

Patterns

Why don’t you + V? · How about + V-ing? · Don’t forget to + V · I’m pleased to + V · Do I have to + V?

Task 3 · language bank

Choose 1–2 expressions from each group

  • Thank + accept

    Thanks for inviting me. · I’d love to come.

  • Time

    What time should I come? · Shall we meet at 10 a.m.?

  • Things to bring

    Do I need to bring anything? · Shall I buy anything?

  • Closing

    See you on Sunday. · Write back soon.

Task 3 · sample answer (show after students write)

Linda’s reply

Hi Mai,

Thank you for inviting me to your house this Sunday. I’m so glad to come and try some recipes from the book with you. Shall we meet at 10 a.m.? Please tell me if I need to buy anything in advance.

See you soon,
Linda
