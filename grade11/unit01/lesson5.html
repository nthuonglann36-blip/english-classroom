Task 3 · check understanding

Quick questions

What do many people think of when they want to improve their health? expensive health products and fitness programmes

Round 2 · fill in the blanks · part 1 (extension)

Opening the TV show

TV host: Good evening! I’m Oliver and you’re watching “Teen Life”, where we answer questions from young people. This week we have received a lot of health questions, so today we have Katherine Jones, a food expert, to talk about healthy eating for teenagers and how food can affect their health.

Katherine: Good evening, and thank you for inviting me.

Round 2 · part 2

Health myth vs simple eating

Katherine: Well, when people want to improve their health and fitness, they often think of expensive health products and fitness programmes. But in fact, we can solve many health problems if we just eat healthily.

Round 2 · part 3

Skin problems and better food

A lot of teenagers suffer from acne, pimples or other skin issues, and often look for expensive skincare products. … Food with a lot of sugar is not very good for your skin, so you should cut down on it. By contrast, brown rice, yoghurt, watermelons, and green vegetables are great for healthy skin.

Round 2 · part 4

Brain, bones and muscles

Green vegetables can improve brain health and memory, and help teens concentrate. Teenagers are still growing so they also need food for building strong bones and muscles. Research shows that eggs, fish, butter, carrots and sweet potatoes can help make them taller and stronger.

Round 2 · part 5

The final message

Although food can’t replace exercise, eating a balanced diet with all the nutrients you need, will definitely help improve your muscle strength.

TV host: I guess “you are what you eat” after all. Thank you, Katherine.
