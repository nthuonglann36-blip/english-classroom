GRADE 11 · UNIT 4 · EXERCISE 4

Lucky Box!

Read. Write.
Reveal. Choose.

Five reading questions · 20 seconds to write

  • Correct answer: +10 base points.
  • Then choose a bonus box.
  • Teacher checks the answers first.

Choose a reading question

NO MORE THAN FOUR words and/or a number.

How to play

  1. Teams write their answer on a mini board in 20 seconds. Use no more than four words and/or a number.
  2. Reveal the correct answer. The teacher marks every team that answered correctly.
  3. Each correct team gets +10 base points, then chooses one face-down bonus box.
  4. Bonus boxes contain −10, −5, +5, +10, +15 or +20 points. Their positions are shuffled for each team.
  5. Finish all confirmed teams’ boxes before leaving the round. The full result is saved once.
  6. After five questions, the highest score wins. Undo restores the latest round; reopening a settled question only reviews it.
