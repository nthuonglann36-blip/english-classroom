📖 Reading article & game guide

🧩 Answer missions

Complete two textbook heading matches, five textbook one-word gaps, and five additional evidence questions. Students can say answers aloud or use the answer cards.

🏗️ Build with points

Teachers give +10 to a team that answers correctly and +5 if it explains the evidence. Buildings automatically appear at 10, 25, 40, 60 and 85 points. No team needs to be selected first.

🏆 Finish the challenge

Choose any of 12 challenge tiles, use hint or timer if desired, and reveal the answer. Tap Announce winner to display the leading team. Undo and Reset are available.

Tip: You can play in textbook order or use the 🎲 button to pick an unfinished question. The two HTML games save progress separately.
