CLASSROOM COINS · TWO TO FOUR TEAMS

How to grow your bank

  1. Each team starts with $1,000. Choose a vault, read the question and discuss the answer.
  2. Every team wagers $100, $200 or $300 before recording answers. You cannot wager more than your balance.
  3. Teams show their answers at the same time. The teacher records each answer, or chooses No answer.
  4. Reveal together. A correct answer wins the wager; an incorrect answer loses the wager. Read the evidence to check why.
  5. At $0, play a recovery turn: a correct answer earns $100; an incorrect answer costs nothing.
  6. After 12 rounds, the team with the most coins wins. Ties are welcome! Use Undo to correct the latest round.

Example: $1,000 − a $200 loss = $800. A $200 win grows $1,000 to $1,200.
