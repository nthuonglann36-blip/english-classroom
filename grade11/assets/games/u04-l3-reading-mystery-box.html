Game rules

  • Divide the class into 2 - 4 teams. Teams take turns choosing a number from 1 to 15 and answering a question.
  • If your team answers correctly, you earn a chance to open a Mystery Box.
  • If wrong, the turn goes to the next player/team.
  • When you get the correct answer, you’ll get a random Mystery Box with secret points. You can choose to keep it or give it to another player/team.
  • After all 15 questions, the player/team with the most points wins!

Choose who gets the box before revealing the points. Boxes can add or subtract points. For open questions, the teacher accepts any suitable answer.
