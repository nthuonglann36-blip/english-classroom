While-writing

Write a proposal (150–180 words)

17 minutes
Task 2 · Write a proposal (150–180 words) to your head teacher · use the ideas in 1 17 min

Outline (1)

Title: A SCHOOL CAMPAIGN AGAINST CYBERBULLYING · To: ____ · Date: ____ · Prepared by: ____

Introduction: … has become a common/serious issue nowadays because … · We would like to propose …

Details about the campaign: The campaign events/activities will take place in/at … The campaign will last for … · … will participate in … · The campaign will target … · The main events/activities will include …

Task 2 · outline with useful expressions

Outline (2)

Goals and benefits of the campaign: (The campaign) aims at …/doing … · (The campaign) will allow (students, teenagers) to do …

Conclusion: We hope that you will consider our proposal …

Task 2 · sample proposal (1)

A school campaign against cyberbullying

To: Ms Le Thi Hoa · Date: 14 February, 20__ · Prepared by: Vu Hoang Ha, President of the Social Awareness Club

Introduction: Cyberbullying has become a common issue nowadays because more students are using digital devices. Moreover, there have been many reports of people making offensive comments about others online. Therefore, we would like to propose a school campaign against cyberbullying.

Task 2 · sample proposal (2)

Details about the campaign

The campaign will include a series of activities organised by the school Social Awareness Club. They will take place in our school hall after classes. The campaign will last for a month, from the 1st of March until the 1st of April. All students who are interested will be able to participate in the activities. The main events will include several talks by cyberbullying experts and sharing sessions for students to talk about their experiences of cyberbullying.

Task 2 · sample proposal (3)

Goals, benefits and conclusion

Goals and benefits: Our goals are to raise awareness of cyberbullying and to promote responsible and safe use of the Internet. The campaign will also help students identify cyberbullying and report it when they see it.

Conclusion: We hope that you will consider our proposal. We believe that this campaign will encourage students to make friends and promote a positive atmosphere in the school, which will ensure cyberbullying does not happen.

Post-writing

Cross-checking

10 minutes
Cross-checking · swap proposals · two stars and one wish

Peer review

  • 📋 title · to · date · prepared by
  • ❗ why the issue is serious
  • 📅 when · where · how long
  • 👥 who participates
  • 🎤 main activities
  • 🎯 goals and benefits
  • 🔗 linking words
  • 📏 150–180 words

Rubric: organisation · legibility · ideas · word choice · grammar and mechanics — each /10 → total /50

Wrap-up · exit ticket 3 min

Before you leave…

1. Name the four main parts of a proposal. introduction · details about the campaign · goals and benefits · conclusion

2. Give one campaign activity and its goal. e.g. talks by experts — to raise awareness of cyberbullying

3. How do you end a proposal politely? We hope that you will consider our proposal.

Wrap-up

Homework
