Pronunciation

Intonation in choice questions

12 minutes
Remember! · page 101

Choice questions

❓ Choice questions are questions that include a list of choices.

↗ Each choice mentioned before the word ‘or’ has rising intonation at the end, while the last one has falling intonation at the end.

🍵 Would you choose tea ↗ or coffee ↘?

Task 1 · Listen and repeat · pay attention to the intonation · practise in pairs 5 min 🎧 Track 70 · 0:43

Listen and repeat

1. Should we focus our campaign on a general social issue ↗ or one affecting mainly teens ↘?

2. Is the top problem facing teens today peer pressure ↗, body shaming ↗, or bullying ↘?

Task 2 · Mark the intonation · then listen and check · practise in pairs 7 min 🎧 Track 71 · 0:56

Mark ↗ or ↘

1. Is peer pressure good or bad? good ↗ or bad ↘

2. Is this a social or environmental issue? social ↗ or environmental issue ↘

3. Should our campaign focus on crime, overpopulation, or climate change? crime ↗, overpopulation ↗, or climate change ↘

4. Do you talk to your parents, share problems with your friends, or ask your teachers for advice? parents ↗, friends ↗, advice ↘

Grammar

Linking words and phrases

13 minutes
Remember! · page 102 · we use linking words and phrases to connect ideas, clauses, or sentences

Linking words and phrases

To add ideas and informationBesides (this/that), … · Moreover, … · In addition (to + noun or gerund), …
To contrast ideasHowever, … · Although …, … · In spite of/Despite (noun or gerund), … · By contrast, …
To give reasonsBecause …, … · Because of (noun or gerund), … · As/Since …, …
To show resultsAs a result, … · As a consequence, … · Therefore, …
Grammar · Task 1 · Connect these sentences, using linking words or phrases 6 min

Connect the sentences (1–2)

1. Some people cannot control their anger when drinking alcohol. ________, they become violent. As a result / As a consequence / Therefore

2. Body shaming is a very common issue. ________, many people are not aware of it. However

Grammar · Task 1 · Connect these sentences, using linking words or phrases

Grammar · Task 2 · Pairs · add more sentences to each item · use different linking words 7 min

Add more sentences
