Task 2 · Match the parts of the article with the descriptions 7 min

Descriptions A–D

A. Introduction – a short paragraph stating the issue and what the article will cover

B. Title – the topic of the article in a few words

C. Body paragraphs – each presenting a main point for or against the issue, supported by facts, examples, or explanations

D. Conclusion – a summary of the main points and sometimes the writer’s opinion

Task 2 · the model article · News World · parts 1–2

Living in a smart city: good or bad?

1 B · title  LIVING IN A SMART CITY: GOOD OR BAD?

2 A · introduction If you live in a smart city, you will probably agree with me that life is not all good as some people may think. So what are the pros and cons of living in a smart city?

Task 2 · the model article · part 3

Living in a smart city: good or bad?

3 C · body: advantages The good thing about smart cities is that they are very modern. Smart technologies make people’s lives easier by reducing household chores. Besides, the city infrastructure which includes sensors and cameras collects information about people and their activities, and uses it to provide faster, cheaper, and better services.

Task 2 · the model article · part 4

Living in a smart city: good or bad?

4 C · body: disadvantages On the other hand, living in a smart city has some disadvantages. Some smart technologies are difficult to use and without training, city dwellers might find them useless. With cameras everywhere in public spaces, people also have limited privacy. They are worried that their personal information might not be protected, and they might become victims of cybercrime.

Task 2 · the model article · part 5

Living in a smart city: good or bad?

5 D · conclusion In conclusion, living in a smart city has both advantages and disadvantages. However, I think these urban areas offer great promise. The issues should be dealt with in the years ahead as the world’s population keeps growing, and more people are moving to big cities.

Language detective · find these in the model article

Useful language

Ask a question: So what are the pros and cons of …?

Introduce a benefit: The good thing about … is that …

Add another idea: Besides, … · In addition, … · Moreover, …

Show contrast: On the other hand, … · However, …

Close and give your opinion: In conclusion, … However, I think …

Task 3 · Write an article (120–150 words) · use the outline 15 min

The advantages and disadvantages of smart cities

Smart cities are built on new technologies to improve people’s lives. The idea of living in one of them sounds very exciting. But is a life controlled by smart technologies good or bad for us?

Let’s start with the advantages. ______

What about the disadvantages? ______

In conclusion, there are both advantages and disadvantages of living in a smart city. In my opinion, ______

Sample article · after you write · paragraphs 2–3

Compare the structure — not just the words

Let’s start with the advantages. Living in a smart city can reduce the negative impact on the environment. Future smart cities will be built in new urban areas with a lot of green space. City dwellers will try to make their cities greener, for example, by growing vegetables in their roof gardens.

What about the disadvantages? When cameras are installed for security purposes, city dwellers will lose their right to privacy. They may feel that someone is watching them all the time.

Sample article · conclusion

Compare the structure — not just the words

In conclusion, there are both advantages and disadvantages of living in a smart city. In my opinion, the benefits are greater, and people will find a way to adapt to the new lifestyle and overcome the challenges.
