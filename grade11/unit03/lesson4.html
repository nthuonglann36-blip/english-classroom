Task 1 · Smart and sustainable cities of the future

Transport · infrastructure · living conditions

🚆 Transport (1) B
Eco-friendly public transport will produce less carbon dioxide or waste, and will be controlled by computers.

🏙️ Infrastructure (2) C
Smart street infrastructure with sensor technology will provide information for faster, cheaper, and better decision-making.

🌱 Living conditions (3) A
People’s lives will be easier because AI technologies will help them do many daily activities.

Tips · page 33

Keep the conversation going with Wh-questions

Wh-questions help you find out important information about people, places, and things — and develop ideas.

Where will people live? · What jobs will they do? · How will technology help city dwellers?

Ask → answer → ask a follow-up → explain.
