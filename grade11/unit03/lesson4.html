SPEAKING FORMULA

Build a stronger answer in three steps

1 • FEATURE

What will there be?

Most people will use electric buses and trams.

2 • HOW IT WORKS

How will it work?

They will be controlled by computers and run on clean energy.

3 • BENEFIT

Why will it help?

As a result, there will be fewer traffic jams and less air pollution.

TIPS • KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING

Don’t stop after one answer

Follow-ups

Ask for more details

  • Really?
  • Why do you think so?
  • How will that work?
  • Can you give an example?

Wh-questions

Develop the idea

  • Where will people live?
  • What jobs will they do?
  • How will technology help city dwellers?

Reacting

Show you are listening

  • That sounds great!
  • I agree with you.
  • That’s true, but…
  • I’m not sure about that.
