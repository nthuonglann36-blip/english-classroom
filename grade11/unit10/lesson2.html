Pronunciation

Intonation in question tags

12 minutes
Remember! · page 111

Question tags: the melody matters

↘ We use falling intonation on the question tag when we are almost sure of the answer and we think that the listener will confirm that our statement is correct, or we want to gently persuade the listener. A: The tiger is an endangered animal, isn’t it? ↘ B: Yes, it is./That’s right.

↘ We can also use falling intonation on the tag to make a point. It’s a beautiful view, isn’t it? ↘

↗ We use rising intonation on the question tag when we are not so sure of the answer. A: You didn’t do your homework, did you? ↗ B: Yes, I did.

Grammar

Compound nouns

13 minutes
Remember! · page 112 · a compound noun is made with two or more words, e.g. air-traffic controller

How compound nouns are built

  • 🔤

    noun + noun

    bus stop

  • 🎨

    adjective + noun

    wildlife

  • 🧺

    -ing form + noun

    washing machine

  • 🎬

    noun + -ing form

    film-making

  • 🏃

    verb + preposition

    break-out

Remember! · page 112 · spelling and plurals

One word, two words or a hyphen?

✍️ Some compound nouns are written as one word (bedroom), some as separate words (tennis shoes), and others with a hyphen (film-maker).

1️⃣ Even if the first noun has a plural meaning, it usually has a singular form, e.g. car park.

➕ To make a compound noun plural, we usually make the second noun plural, e.g. car parks. There are some exceptions, e.g. clothes shop, passers-by.
