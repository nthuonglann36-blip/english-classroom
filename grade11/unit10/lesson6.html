While-writing

Write an opinion essay (150–180 words)

16 minutes
Task 2 · Should we spend more money on restoring local ecosystems? · use the ideas in 1 and the outline 16 min

Outline with useful expressions

Introduction: Today, many people argue that … · From my point of view/In my opinion, this is/people should …

Body: Firstly, the most important reason why we should/should not … is that … · Secondly, we should/should not … because/as/since … (this) has caused … That is why … · Finally, … this has led/affected …

Conclusion: In conclusion, I firmly believe that … it is essential to …

Task 2 · sample essay (1)

Restoring local ecosystems

Today many people argue that we should spend more money on restoring local ecosystems. From my point of view, this is a great idea for the following reasons.

Firstly, the most important reason why we should invest in restoring them is that we are already suffering from the impact of their damage. Both air pollution and water pollution have increased and are affecting our health.

Task 2 · sample essay (2)

Restoring local ecosystems

Secondly, habitat loss has caused the disappearance of many plant and animal species. This has affected the balance of local ecosystems since all living things play an important role. When plants or animals die out, the food chain may also break down. That is why we should spend more money on protecting and restoring all wildlife.

Task 2 · sample essay (3)

Restoring local ecosystems

Finally, much of our local forest has been cut down to make space for houses and farm land. This has led to more natural disasters in the region. For example, floods have become more common. They destroy people’s houses, fields, and crops every year. To prevent damage from natural disasters, we should spend more money on planting more trees and restoring our forest.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that it is essential to spend more money on restoring local ecosystems. Investing in nature conservation will help create a healthy and stable living environment for future generations.

Post-writing

Peer review

12 minutes
Peer review · swap essays · two stars and one wish

Check your partner’s essay

  • 🗣️ a clear opinion in the introduction
  • 1️⃣ Firstly … 2️⃣ Secondly … 3️⃣ Finally …
  • 💡 a reason + an example for each point
  • 🔗 because · since · That is why · This has led to …
  • ✅ In conclusion, I firmly believe …
  • 📏 150–180 words

Rubric: organisation · legibility · ideas · word choice · grammar and mechanics — each /10 → total /50

Wrap-up · exit ticket 3 min

Before you leave…

1. Give one idea FOR restoring local ecosystems. Many plant and animal species are disappearing. · Pollution is affecting our health.

2. Give one idea AGAINST it. It is more important to invest in healthcare and education.

3. How can you start your conclusion? In conclusion, I firmly believe that …
