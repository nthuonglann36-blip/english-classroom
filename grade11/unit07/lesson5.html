Warm-up

Welcome to the cooking lab

5 minutes
Task 1 · Pairs · look at the picture and discuss the questions 5 min

What vocational course is this?

1. What kind of vocational course are they taking? a cooking course

2. Do you think students need any special qualifications to apply for this course? No — they just need to be passionate about food and cooking.

3. What do you think students expect to learn from this course? knife skills · sauces · plating dishes · food safety

Post-listening

Would you be interested in a cooking course?

10 minutes
Task 5 · Pairs · discuss the following questions 10 min

Would you be interested in a cooking course? Why / Why not? If yes, what kind?

🍳 I’d be interested in a … course because …

⏱️ The short course / professional course would suit me because …

👨‍🍳 I like the fact that this course also provides …

🙅 I wouldn’t choose it because …

Task 5 · sample answer

A cooking course? Yes, please!

I’d be interested in a cooking course because cooking has always been my hobby. In addition, I think the time of training is quite short, and the cost is also affordable for me and my family.

I like the fact that this course also provides apprenticeship opportunities. This will help me find a suitable job immediately after I finish the course.

Wrap-up · exit ticket · two minutes 3 min

Before you leave…

1. What is an apprentice? a person working for an employer to learn a skill or a job

2. How long does a professional cooking course at ABC last? two years

3. Where can parents and students find information about the school? in the (school) brochure

4. One question you would ask a school receptionist… e.g. How long does the course last? · Do you have a brochure?
