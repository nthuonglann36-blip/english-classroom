Content words · STRESSED

Grammar

Present and past participle clauses

13 minutes
Remember! · present participle clauses · page 54

V-ing … , S + V … (active meaning, same subject)

Reason for an action

Trapping too much of the sun’s heat, greenhouse gases stop it from escaping back into space.

Two actions at the same time

Walking on the beach, they picked up litter.

Task 1 · Find and correct the mistakes 6 min

Present participle clauses

1. Was waiting for the train, I read some articles about global warming. Was waiting → Waiting

2. Saw the forest fire, the children immediately called the police. Saw → Seeing

3. The students took part in green activities, were planting more than 100 trees. were planting → planting

Remember! · past participle clauses · page 54

V3 / -ed … , S + V … (passive meaning)

Reason

Worried about climate change, many young people joined the environmental protests.

Condition

Burnt for energy, fossil fuels release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.

Task 2 · Rewrite using past participle clauses 7 min

Keep the meaning

Example: We were given enough information about the causes of global warming, so we started our report. → Given enough information about the causes of global warming, we started our report.

1. If farm animals are not kept cool in hot weather, they can suffer from heat stress. Not kept cool in hot weather, …

Task 2 · Rewrite using past participle clauses

Keep the meaning

2. Some farmers were worried about the consequences of deforestation, so they stopped burning trees to create farmland. Worried about the consequences of deforestation, …

3. If carbon dioxide is produced in huge amounts, it causes air pollution and climate change. Produced in huge amounts, …

-ing or V3 / -ed? · choose by meaning

Active or passive?

-ing · ACTIVE

The subject does the action.
Trapping heat, greenhouse gases warm the planet.

V3 / -ed · PASSIVE

Something is done to the subject.
Burnt for energy, coal releases CO₂.

  • protect by new laws + endangered species recover
