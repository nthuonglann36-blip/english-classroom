Task 2 · True or False? 8 min

Read again and decide

Task 3 · notice

One verb does most of the work

do the laundry · do the heavy lifting · do the washing-up

do covers most chores — but the rubbish gets put out, and groceries get shopped for.

So what do we say about the dishes after dinner? do the washing-up

Task 4 · Verb forms 5 min

Complete the sentences

1. I’d love to, but I’m afraid I can’t. I (prepare) ’m preparing dinner.

2. My mum usually (do) does the cooking, but she (work) ’s working late today.

Why two different forms in one sentence? usually = her routine → present simple. today = happening around now, not her habit → present continuous.

Grammar spotlight

Routine or right now?

Present simple

What is always true — habits, routines, family rules.

My mum does the cooking.
I never do the cooking.
My sister does the laundry.

usually often never

Present continuous

What is happening around now — today only, not the rule.

I’m preparing dinner.
She’s working late today.
She’s studying for her exams.

now today this evening
