Task 1 · Look and predict

Open your book at page 13

📖 Textbook, page 13

Look at the Task 1 picture before you listen.

Who are they? Where are they? A student and a talk-show host, in a TV studio. The show is called Family Life.

What is around the boy’s neck? A gold medal.

So what will they talk about? His family, and how they helped him succeed.

Task 2 · Check your guess 6 min 🎧 Track 07 · 1:35

Listen to the opening — were you right?

What is the interview about? Family life — and how his family helped him achieve academic success.

Listening tip

On the first listening, write nothing. Just catch the main idea. Take notes on the second.

Task 3 · True or False? 10 min

Listen again and decide

  • Only his father works to support the family. FALSE Both parents work.
  • His mother does all the housework alone. FALSE He helps with the chores too.
  • His family encourages him when he has problems. TRUE
  • Family routines help him feel supported. TRUE
Task 4 · Fill the gaps 8 min

One word in each gap

1. My family helps me overcome ______. difficulties

2. They always show me their ______ and support. love

3. They cheer me up when I feel ______. sad
