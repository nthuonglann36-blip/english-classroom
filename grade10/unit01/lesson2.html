4

Grammar

Present simple vs present continuous

13 minutes
Remember

Two tenses, two jobs

Present simple

Habits · routines · what is always true.

She does the cooking every day.
I never do the washing-up.

always usually often never every day

Present continuous

Happening now, or around now — not the rule.

She is doing the cooking right now.
They are studying this week.

now at the moment today this week

Task 1 · Choose the correct form 4 min

Simple or continuous?

1. My sister ______ (do) the laundry every Saturday. does

2. Look! Nam ______ (put out) the rubbish now. is putting out

3. Dad ______ (clean) the house at the weekend. cleans

4. Be quiet — Lan ______ (study) for her exams. is studying

5. Who ______ (do) the washing-up in your family? does

Task 2 · Complete the text 4 min

A busy evening at home

In my family, everyone helps. My mum usually does the cooking, but tonight she isn’t doing it — she is tired. Dad is watching the news while my brother and I are doing the washing-up. My sister is tidying up the living room, and the cat is trying to help — but she isn’t helping at all!

Notice

usually → simple. tonight, while, right now → continuous.

Game · 20 Questions 5 min

Guess what I am doing

Yes / No questions only!

One student thinks of a chore. The class asks up to 20 questions to guess it.

Are you doing it now? · Do you do it every day? · Are you using water? · Is it in the kitchen?

Rule: every question must use the present simple or the present continuous. Put two columns on the board — Do you…? and Are you…? — so students can see at a glance which tense they are using.
