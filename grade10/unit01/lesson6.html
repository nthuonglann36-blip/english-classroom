Paragraph structure 4 min

Five moves, in this order

  • 1️⃣

    Topic sentence

    My family has a few routines that keep us close.

  • 2️⃣

    First,…

    routine 1 + why it matters

  • 3️⃣

    Second,…

    routine 2 + why it matters

  • 4️⃣

    Finally,…

    routine 3 + why it matters

  • 5️⃣

    Concluding sentence

    These routines bring us closer together.

Write your own

120–150 words · Task 3, page 15

18 minutes
Model paragraph

What a good answer looks like

First, my family always have breakfast together every morning. Breakfast is usually a quick meal with bread or noodles, but it provides a great opportunity to sit down, eat healthy food, and share our plans for the day. Second, we spend Saturday evenings watching our favourite film together. We often share snacks and lively discussions, which helps us understand each other better. Finally, on the second Sunday of every month, we visit our grandparents. We help them with household chores like gardening and house cleaning, and then enjoy a warm family lunch. These routines bring us closer together and make our family bonds stronger.

Count it: 118 words. Each routine gives what + how often + why it matters. Drop the third part and the paragraph turns into a list.
