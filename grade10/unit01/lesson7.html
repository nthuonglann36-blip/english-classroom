Useful expressions 4 min

Two levels of opinion

Strong

I strongly believe that…
I’m sure that…
I have no doubt that…

Use it when you have a solid reason and will defend it.

Neutral

In my opinion,…
To my mind,…
I suppose that…

Use it when you are offering a view, not a certainty.

Useful expressions 4 min

One more thing

Notice

Saying I strongly believe and then failing to give a reason costs you more than saying I suppose from the start. Pick the level you actually mean.

Task 2 · Pair role-play 5 min

Should family members spend time together?

One of you takes the strong level, one the neutral. Then swap.

A: I strongly believe families should eat dinner together every day.
B: To my mind, that’s hard when parents work late — but once or twice a week is possible.

Three topics to choose from: Eating dinner together · A family weekend outing · Watching one film together instead of everyone on their own phone

Culture · Family Values

Family Values

Based on surveys reported by Daniel Yankelovich in Values and Public Policy – 11 points that a majority of Americans agree are “family values.”

6 Traditional Values

  • Respecting one's parents
  • Being responsible for one's actions
  • Having faith in God
  • Respecting authority
  • Married to the same person for life
  • Leaving the world in better shape

5 Blended Values

(traditional + newer, more expressive)

  • Giving emotional support to other members of the family
  • Respecting people for themselves
  • Developing greater skill in communicating one's feelings
  • Respecting one's children
  • Living up to one's potential as an individual
Task 2 · Compare 10 min

Do Vietnamese families share these values?

The same

All five values are there in Vietnamese families too — honesty, respect for elders, table manners, please and thank you, helping with chores.

Ours in particular

Filial piety — hiếu thảo
Ancestor respect — thờ cúng tổ tiên
Several generations under one roof

10.A2.1 The hard question: which values should stay as they are, and which should change with the times? There is no right answer. But you must give a reason — “because I like it” does not count. And let your partner finish before you push back.
