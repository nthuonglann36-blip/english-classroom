How to play

  1. Choose a question. Answer it aloud. The teacher checks your answer before you draw a card.
  2. The number on the cards is the point you can get. Number cards add 1–9 points to your team score.
  3. KEEP THE POINTS OR DRAW AGAIN FOR MORE POINTS. Choose Keep to end your round, or Draw again to take another card.
  4. SWAP: you must swap your score with one opponent. Choose which team. Then keep the new score or draw again.
  5. SKIP: choose one opponent to miss their next turn. Then keep the score or draw again.
  6. −4: you lose 4 points. Then keep the score or draw again. Scores can go below zero.
  7. RESET: you lose all your points. Your score becomes 0 and the round ends.

Points change as each card is revealed. Keeping ends the round without adding the same points again. The highest score after 15 questions wins.
