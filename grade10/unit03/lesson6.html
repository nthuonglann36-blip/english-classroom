Blog Builder

Team game · order and edit

6 minutes
Blog Builder · round 1 · order the strips 3 min

Put the six sentences in order

A We went to the open-air stage just before seven.

B Last Saturday, I went to Sunshine Music Night with my classmates.

C I hope to go again next year!

D The band played three fantastic songs, and we sang along.

E I felt excited because the crowd was friendly and cheerful.

F The festival was in a large city park near my school.

Write the order on your mini board. B → F → A → D → E → C

Blog Builder · round 2 · be the proofreader 3 min

Find and correct FIVE mistakes

Last saturday, I go to the music festival with my friends. The singer perform three songs. We was very excited. I hope visit again next year.

Five edits: Saturday · went · performed · were · hope to visit

Task 3 · Write a blog · page 34 14 min

Imagine you went to a music event

Write a blog (about 120 words) to share your experience. Use the notes in 1 and the words and phrases in 2.

  • ✓ event
  • ✓ when and where
  • ✓ who with
  • ✓ atmosphere
  • ✓ what you did
  • ✓ how you felt
Task 3 · Writing frame

Beginning · body · ending

Beginning: Last ______, I went to ______ with ______. The event took place ______.

Body: The atmosphere was ______. First, we ______. Then, we ______. My favourite part was ______ because ______.

Ending: I felt ______. It was a/an ______ experience. I would love to ______ again.

Linking words: First, Then, After that, Also, Finally, because · Use the past simple: went, saw, had, felt, took.

Task 3 · Sample sentences

Write first — then compare

Opening: when, where, who with Last Saturday, I visited Green Park with my classmates for a music festival.

Event: the best performance A talented young band performed live, and the audience sang along.

Feeling: how and why I felt excited because the atmosphere was lively and friendly.

Closing: final opinion I would love to return next year, so I strongly recommend this festival.
