What kind of TV show is it?

  • six participants
  • dress up and perform
  • judges give scores
  • the audience votes in the final
  • the winner receives a cash prize

Guess the kind of show. Do you know any shows like this? A TV music show / talent show — e.g. America’s Got Talent, Pop Idol, Vietnam’s Got Talent, The X Factor.

Task 1 · Read about a TV music show · page 32 3 min

I know your face

Each week, six participants will dress up and perform as famous international or local artists in a live show. Two main judges and a guest artist will give their scores. After five weeks, only the three participants with the highest scores will remain on the show. On the final night, TV audiences can vote for their preferred performance and decide on the winner and two runners-up. The winner of the show will receive a cash prize.

Speaking toolbox

Organise your show with will + V

The show

Name: Our show is called …

Idea: It is a TV music show in which …

Participants: There will be … participants.

The result

Judges: The judges will give scores for …

Winner: The winner will be decided by …

Prize: The winner will receive …

Task 3 · Present your show · page 32 10 min

Useful expressions

– We came up with an idea about a TV music show which is …

– Let us give you an example.

– In our show, there will be …

– The participants have to …

– The winner will receive …

Share roles: Host (name + idea) · Producer (participants, judges) · Announcer (how to win, prize).
