# Grade 10 · Unit 4 · Language: Grammar discovery

The teacher's requested standalone activity is published unchanged as
`assets/games/u04-grammar-discovery.html` (SHA-256 `34dac064cb72ff2695c5e11ca622258dd15fc04b64d8c48cb80d5f0d835e82cc`).
It is one file with inline CSS, JavaScript and SVG, with no external assets,
libraries, fonts or network requests.

## Lesson integration

- Open from **Grammar discovery ↗** in the Lesson 2 toolbar, or from the
  **A Sunday of surprises** slide before textbook grammar practice.
- The activity opens in a new tab to retain the full projector height and one
  set of navigation controls. Return to the lesson tab for textbook tasks.
- The former opening grammar rule slide is replaced by the discovery launcher.
  The cover and objectives do not name the target tenses before discovery.
- Keep textbook tasks, pronunciation Tracks 29/30, vocabulary and Word Heroes.
  Discovery can also be taught separately; pacing depends on student discussion.

## Activity behavior

There are 23 screens across eight steps. Next unlocks after the current task;
incorrect answers show a hint and allow retry. The teacher can reveal answers.
Tense names and rules first appear at Step 6. Grammar colours appear after
students identify the actions. Timelines include the continuing action and a
parallel-action case, with a reduced-motion alternative.

Check-yourself question 1 accepts **When**, with the teacher's requested
explanation that **while** is the more usual choice in that example.
Writing uses two drafts and three self-check boxes. It is not automatically
graded; the model answer does not alter students' drafts or tick their boxes.
