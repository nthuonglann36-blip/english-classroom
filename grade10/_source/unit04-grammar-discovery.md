## Activity behavior

There are 23 screens across eight steps. Next unlocks after the current task;
incorrect answers show a hint and allow retry. The teacher can reveal answers.
Tense names and rules first appear at Step 6. Grammar colours appear after
students identify the actions. Timelines include the continuing action and a
parallel-action case, with a reduced-motion alternative.

Step 4 questions refer explicitly to the first or second displayed sentence.
At Step 6, students compare the same painting story with **when** introducing
the short event or the action in progress. They discover that when can
introduce either action. The summary includes four story patterns, including
**when + past continuous, past simple**, and explains that the tense matches
the action rather than a fixed rule for when.

Check-yourself question 1 accepts **When** and **While** equally, with the
same explanation: the washing was already in progress. No tense names or
rules are introduced before Step 6.
Writing uses two drafts and three self-check boxes. It is not automatically
graded; the model answer does not alter students' drafts or tick their boxes.
