A model letter

Task 1 · page 48

8 minutes
Task 1 · Read the application letter · page 48 8 min

Paragraph 1 · Paragraph 2

I am writing to apply for a volunteer position at the local centre for community development. I heard about this opportunity in a public announcement last Monday.

I am interested in your community development projects because I really care about the life of people in the community and want to offer my services to you. Currently, all my Sunday afternoons are free, and I will be able to help at the centre if I am chosen.

Task 1 · Read the application letter · page 48

Paragraph 3 · ending

I have already filled in the online application form and sent it in as requested. I am available for an interview on any weekday after 4.30 p.m. or at weekends. If my application is successful, I can start immediately.

I look forward to hearing from you.
Yours faithfully,
Nguyen Ha Vi

Language bank · useful expressions

APPLY → WHY → WHEN

  • APPLY

    I am writing to apply for … · I heard about this opportunity in / on / from …

  • WHY

    I am interested in … because … · I really care about … · I believe I am suitable because I am …

  • WHEN

    I am available for an interview … · If my application is successful, I can start …

  • ENDING

    I look forward to hearing from you. · Yours faithfully,

Task 3 · Sample answer

Compare after you write

I am writing to apply for a volunteer position at the head office of Heart to Heart Charitable Organisation. I saw the job advert on our school notice board.

I am interested in volunteer work and projects to help people in our community. I am polite, reliable and hardworking. I also have great people skills, which I believe are important for this position.

I am available for an interview on any weekday after 4.30 p.m. or at weekends. If my application is successful, I can start next month.

Post-writing · be the recruiter 6 min

Would you hire this applicant?

  • ☐ position mentioned
  • ☐ where they saw the advert
  • ☐ a clear reason
  • ☐ suitable qualities
  • ☐ interview time
  • ☐ start date
  • ☐ polite ending

Swap letters with a partner, tick the checklist and suggest one improvement.

Wrap-up · exit ticket

An application letter has three main paragraphs

Paragraph 1 — the job + where you saw the advert

Paragraph 2 — why you want the job

Paragraph 3 — when you can have an interview and start work

Homework: rewrite your letter in your notebook; prepare Lesson 7 — Communication and Culture (page 49).
