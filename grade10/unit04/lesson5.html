Useful words · Listening · 1 / 17

announcement

(n) /əˈnaʊnsmənt/ thông báo The announcement asks for more volunteers.
Useful words · Listening · 2 / 17

position

(n) /pəˈzɪʃn/ vị trí công việc, chức vụ I am applying for a volunteer position.
Useful words · Listening · 3 / 17

spare

(v/adj) /speə(r)/ dành ra (v); rảnh, dư (adj) v: Can you spare two hours?
adj: I volunteer in my spare time.
Useful words · Listening · 4 / 17

non-profit

(adj) /ˌnɒn ˈprɒfɪt/ phi lợi nhuận The centre is a non-profit organisation.
Useful words · Listening · 5 / 17

organisation

(n) /ˌɔːɡənaɪˈzeɪʃn/ tổ chức This organisation supports local families.
Useful words · Listening · 6 / 17

support

(n/v) /səˈpɔːt/ sự hỗ trợ (n); hỗ trợ, ủng hộ (v) n: Our club offers support.
v: We support local families.
Useful words · Listening · 7 / 17

job training course

(np) /ˌdʒɒb ˈtreɪnɪŋ kɔːs/ khoá đào tạo nghề The centre offers a job training course.
Useful words · Listening · 8 / 17

organise

(v) /ˈɔːɡənaɪz/ tổ chức, sắp xếp The centre will organise training courses.
Useful words · Listening · 9 / 17

look for

(phr. v) /lʊk fɔː(r)/ tìm kiếm I look for volunteer jobs at the local centre.
Useful words · Listening · 10 / 17

train

(v) /treɪn/ đào tạo, huấn luyện Staff train volunteers to sort donations.
Useful words · Listening · 11 / 17

similar

(adj) /ˈsɪmɪlə(r)/ tương tự, giống nhau The volunteers have similar interests.
Useful words · Listening · 12 / 17

deadline

(n) /ˈdedlaɪn/ hạn chót Send your application before the deadline.
Useful words · Listening · 13 / 17

generous

(adj) /ˈdʒenərəs/ hào phóng, rộng lượng Local people are generous with donations.
Useful words · Listening · 14 / 17

donation

(n) /dəʊˈneɪʃn/ sự quyên góp; đồ hoặc khoản quyên góp Your donation of books can help local children.
Useful words · Listening · 15 / 17

lonely

(adj) /ˈləʊnli/ cô đơn Volunteers visit people who feel lonely.
Useful words · Listening · 16 / 17

facility

(n) /fəˈsɪləti/ cơ sở, tiện nghi The centre has a modern training facility.
Useful words · Listening · 17 / 17

application form

(np) /ˌæplɪˈkeɪʃn fɔːm/ mẫu đơn đăng ký, đơn ứng tuyển Please fill in the application form.

Task 3 · Listen again · true or false? · page 47 7 min 🎧 Track 32 · 1:43

Correct the false statements · 1–3

  • This organisation helps people in the area. TRUE
  • Only poor people can get support from the City Centre for Community Development. FALSE Teenagers, lonely old people and others also get support.
  • The centre is looking for volunteers now. TRUE

Correct the false statements · 4–5
