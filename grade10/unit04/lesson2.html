What does -less mean?

Word family · Trap 1

The rule and two traps

-ful = having
-less = without

TRAP 1

I was hopeless at maths. = very bad at maths

Word family · Trap 2

helpful and helpless

TRAP 2

helpful is NOT the opposite of helpless.

The opposite of helpful is unhelpful.

helpless = not able to do anything to help yourself

Grammar

You write the rule!

past simple · past continuous

We use the past continuous for a past action in progress (longer action).

We use the past simple for an action which interrupted it (shorter action).

Grammar

Remember!

Past continuous

an action in progress in the past (longer action).

Past simple

an action which interrupted it (shorter action).

Grammar · Choose by meaning

Remember!
