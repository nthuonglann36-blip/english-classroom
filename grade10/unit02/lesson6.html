Paragraph structure

Five moves, in this order

  • 1️⃣

    Topic sentence

    There are several practical actions we can take…

  • 2️⃣

    First,…

    suggestion 1 + For example,…

  • 3️⃣

    Second,…

    suggestion 2 + result

  • 4️⃣

    Finally,…

    suggestion 3 + For instance,…

  • 5️⃣

    In conclusion,…

    one sentence that ties it together

3

Write

120–150 words · Task 3, page 25

8 minutes
Model paragraph

What a good answer looks like

There are several practical actions we can take to improve the environment in our school. First, we should organise regular clean-up days. For example, students can clean classrooms and pick up rubbish in the schoolyard every Friday afternoon. Second, planting more green trees around the campus will help absorb carbon dioxide and create fresh air. Finally, the school should set up more recycling bins in common areas, such as the canteen and the sports ground. For instance, clearly labelled bins for plastic, paper and organic waste will make sorting much easier. In conclusion, these simple measures will make our school greener and cleaner.

Each suggestion gives what to do + what it achieves. Drop the second half and the paragraph turns into a to-do list.

Peer correction

Mark it, do not rewrite it

15 minutes
Marking symbols

Five symbols, no rewriting

SymbolMeans
spspelling
Twrong tense
WOword order
^a word is missing
Ccapital letter
Marking symbols

Why these five?

Mark the symbol in the margin — do not write the correction. If you write the correction, your partner copies it and learns nothing. The symbol tells them where to look; finding the fix is their job.
