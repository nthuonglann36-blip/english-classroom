Carbon footprint

Watch, then answer

5 minutes
Warm-up

Where do the emissions come from?

Name the three biggest sources. Energy use at home · transport · food production.

So whose carbon footprint is it — yours, or the factory’s? Both. The factory burns the fuel, but somebody buys what it makes. That is why personal choices count — and why they are not the whole answer.

Useful expressions 3 min

One more thing

Notice

How about…? is the softest — it offers instead of telling. Use it with someone who did not ask for your advice.

Task 2 · Pair role-play 5 min

Ask a friend for green advice

Ask · get advice · say what you will actually do.

A: My electricity bill is huge. What should I do?
B: How about turning off the appliances at the wall? I advise you to start with the TV.
A: Good idea — I’ll try that tonight.

10.C3.2 Now write the same exchange as two chat messages. Online, advice with no greeting reads as an order. Keep How about…? and add a reason — the tone of voice is missing in text.

Task 2 · Pair discussion 8 min

How will you shrink yours?

“To reduce my carbon footprint, I will cycle to school instead of asking for motorbike rides, turn off the air conditioner when I do not need it, and stop buying single-use plastic bottles.”

Notice: three actions, all in the future, all things one student can actually do.
